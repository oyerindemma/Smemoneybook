# SME MoneyBook User Manual and Video Guide

This guide explains how a business owner, staff member, or accountant can use SME MoneyBook. It is written so it can also be used as a video walkthrough script.

## Video Overview

- **Audience:** Nigerian SME owners and staff who record daily sales, expenses, stock, debts, and reports.
- **Goal:** Help a first-time user create an account, set up a business, record money activity, manage customers/suppliers, track stock, use reports, and understand the More tools.
- **Suggested video length:** 12 to 18 minutes.
- **Demo account:** Use a test or staging account. Do not show real customer phone numbers, invoices, or private business data.

## 1. Welcome Screen and Account Setup

### What The User Sees

The first screen introduces SME MoneyBook with the message: **Track your business money daily without stress.** The user can select **Start Free** or **I already have an account**.

### Manual Steps

1. Open the SME MoneyBook app.
2. Tap **Start Free** to create a new account.
3. Enter your name, email address, and password.
4. Tap **Continue**.
5. If you already have an account, tap **I already have an account**, enter your email and password, then tap **Continue**.

### Video Script

"Welcome to SME MoneyBook. This app helps you record business money every day without accounting stress. To begin, tap Start Free. Enter your name, email, and password, then continue. If you already have an account, choose I already have an account and sign in."

## 2. Business Onboarding

### What The User Sees

After registration, the app asks what type of business the user runs. Available options include Fashion, Food business, POS business, Retail shop, Pharmacy, Freelancer, Beauty salon, Electronics, Logistics, and Other.

### Manual Steps

1. Select your business type.
2. Enter your business name if you want. This is optional.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Choose what you want help with, such as tracking sales, tracking expenses, knowing profit, sending invoices, managing customers, or business reports.
5. Tap **Record my first transaction**.

### Video Script

"Next, choose the type of business you run. SME MoneyBook uses this to prepare useful expense categories for you. You can also enter your business name, but it is optional. After that, choose your main goal, such as tracking sales, knowing profit, or managing customers. Then tap Record my first transaction."

## 3. Record The First Transaction

### Manual Steps

1. Choose **Add Sale** if money came into the business.
2. Choose **Add Expense** if the business spent money.
3. Enter the amount.
4. For a sale, enter the customer name if needed.
5. For an expense, select or enter the category where available.
6. Add a short note if useful.
7. Save the transaction.
8. Tap **Go to dashboard** when the setup is complete.

### Video Script

"Your first record can be a sale or an expense. For example, if you sold goods worth 25,000 naira, choose Add Sale and enter 25000. If the customer name matters, add it. For an expense, choose Add Expense, enter the amount, and select the expense category. Save it, and the app will update your money totals immediately."

## 4. Main Money Dashboard

### What The User Sees

The Money screen shows today's sales, today's expenses, today's profit, available money, unpaid invoices, weekly growth, spending warnings, and business health.

The bottom navigation has:

- **Money**
- **People**
- **Record**
- **Stock**
- **More**

### Manual Steps

1. Open **Money** from the bottom navigation.
2. Review today's sales, expenses, profit, and available money.
3. Use **Quick actions** to add sales, add expenses, send invoices, or receive payment.
4. Scroll down to view activity, progress prompts, reminders, and previous transactions.
5. If you made a wrong entry and have permission, use the correction/reversal action in the activity feed.

### Video Script

"This is the Money dashboard. At the top, you can see how your business is doing today. SME MoneyBook shows today's sales, expenses, profit, and available money. Use the quick actions to add a sale, add an expense, send an invoice, or receive a payment. The bottom menu lets you move between Money, People, Stock, and More. The center Record button is the fastest way to add a new money record."

## 5. Add A Sale

### Manual Steps

1. Tap **Record** or **Add Sale**.
2. Make sure **Add Sale** is selected.
3. If you are selling a stock item, select the item under **Goods sold** and enter the quantity.
4. Enter the amount if it is not filled from stock.
5. Choose the money location, such as Cash, Bank, or POS.
6. Leave **Customer paid now** checked if the customer has paid.
7. If the customer will pay later, uncheck **Customer paid now**.
8. For pay-later sales, enter the customer name, phone or WhatsApp number, and optional due date.
9. Add an invoice note if needed.
10. Tap **Save sale** or **Save invoice**.

### Video Script

"To record a sale, tap Record and choose Add Sale. Enter the amount and choose where the money went, such as Cash, Bank, or POS. If the customer has paid already, leave Customer paid now turned on. If the customer will pay later, turn it off, enter the customer name and phone number, and add a due date if needed. Saving a pay-later sale creates an invoice and adds the customer to People."

## 6. Add An Expense

### Manual Steps

1. Tap **Record** or **Add Expense**.
2. Select **Add Expense**.
3. Enter the expense amount.
4. Choose a category.
5. Select the money location.
6. Leave **Paid already** checked if the expense has been paid.
7. If you will pay the supplier later, uncheck **Paid already** and enter supplier details where requested.
8. Add a note if useful.
9. Tap **Save expense**.

### Video Script

"To record spending, tap Record and choose Add Expense. Enter the amount, pick the expense category, and choose where the money came from. If you have already paid, leave Paid already selected. If you will pay later, turn it off so SME MoneyBook can track the supplier bill."

## 7. Voice Entry

### Manual Steps

1. On the Money dashboard, tap the microphone button beside **Add Sale** or **Add Expense**.
2. Speak the transaction clearly, such as "sale 5000 cash" or "expense 2000 transport".
3. Review the draft that appears.
4. Correct the amount, account, category, or note if needed.
5. Save the record.

### Video Script

"On supported browsers, especially Android Chrome, you can use voice entry. Tap the microphone beside Add Sale or Add Expense, say the transaction, then review the draft before saving. Always check the amount and money location before you save."

## 8. People: Customers Owing And Supplier Bills

### What The User Sees

The People page shows customers who owe the business and supplier bills the business needs to pay.

### Manual Steps For Customer Debt

1. Open **People**.
2. Look under **Customers owing**.
3. Tap a customer invoice to view details.
4. Use **Send WhatsApp Reminder** to remind the customer.
5. Use **Send invoice** to send invoice details.
6. Use **Confirm payment** after payment is received.
7. To record money collected, enter the amount and select the account where the money was received.
8. Tap **Confirm collection**.

### Manual Steps For Supplier Bills

1. Open **People**.
2. Look under **Supplier bills**.
3. Select the payment account.
4. Enter the amount paid, or leave blank to settle the remaining amount where supported.
5. Tap **Pay**.
6. Use reminder notes, WhatsApp, or SMS where available.

### Video Script

"The People screen helps you control debt. Customers owing are people who bought from you but have not paid yet. Tap an invoice to view the amount due, send a WhatsApp reminder, send the invoice, or confirm payment. When the customer pays, select the account that received the money and confirm collection. Supplier bills work the other way: they show what your business still needs to pay."

## 9. Stock Management

### What The User Sees

The Stock page shows product count, quantity, low stock count, stock cost value, potential revenue, and potential profit.

### Manual Steps To Add A Product

1. Open **Stock**.
2. Tap **Add product**.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Enter cost price.
5. Enter selling price.
6. Enter current quantity.
7. Enter the low stock alert level.
8. Tap **Save product**.

### Manual Steps To Adjust Stock

1. Open **Stock**.
2. Go to **Adjust stock**.
3. Select the product.
4. Choose **Stock in** for new stock or **Stock out** for removed stock.
5. Enter the quantity.
6. Add a reason if needed, such as "New delivery" or "Damaged".
7. Tap **Update stock**.

### Manual Steps To Sell From Stock

1. Tap **Record** or go to **Stock** and choose **Create invoice**.
2. Choose **Add Sale**.
3. Select the product under **Goods sold**.
4. Enter the quantity sold.
5. Confirm the amount.
6. Save the sale or invoice.

### Video Script

"The Stock screen helps you know what products you have and what they are worth. Add each product with its cost price, selling price, quantity, and low stock alert level. When new goods arrive, use Stock in. When items are damaged, removed, or adjusted manually, use Stock out. When you sell a product from the sales form, SME MoneyBook reduces stock automatically and calculates profit from the cost price."

## 10. Low Stock Alerts

### Manual Steps

1. Open **Stock**.
2. Scroll to **Low stock alerts**.
3. Review products that are low or out of stock.
4. Tap **Restock** to jump to the stock adjustment form.
5. Tap **Notify owner** to send a stock alert after entering the owner WhatsApp phone number.

### Video Script

"Low stock alerts show products that need attention. If a product is low, tap Restock to add more quantity. You can also notify the owner by WhatsApp when stock needs attention."

## 11. Reports

### What The User Sees

Reports show a simple business summary for daily, weekly, or monthly periods. The app includes money in, cash received, pay-later sales, profit, money out, VAT estimate, receivables aging, payables aging, customer debt, supplier bills, transaction count, top product, and plain-language notes.

### Manual Steps

1. Open **More**.
2. Tap **Reports**.
3. Tap **View details** if the report details are hidden.
4. Choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
5. For daily or weekly reports, select a date.
6. For monthly reports, select the month and year.
7. Review money in, cash received, pay-later sales, profit, money out, and VAT estimate.
8. Check receivables and payables aging.
9. Read the plain notes.
10. Use **Save VAT summary** or **Save snapshot** if needed.
11. Use **Share on WhatsApp** to share a report summary.
12. CSV and PDF export may require upgrade access.

### Video Script

"Reports turn your daily records into simple business summaries. Choose daily, weekly, or monthly, then review sales, expenses, profit, VAT estimate, debts, and plain notes. These reports are generated from the records you entered, so check them carefully before using them for official filing or major decisions."

## 12. AI Assistant

### Manual Steps

1. Open **More**.
2. Tap **AI Assistant**.
3. Choose a suggested question or type your own.
4. Example questions include:
   - "How much did I make today?"
   - "Who owes me money?"
   - "What stock is low?"
   - "Summarize this month"
   - "Explain my VAT estimate"
5. Wait for the assistant to answer using your business records.

### Video Script

"The MoneyBook Assistant can answer simple questions about your records. You can ask how much you made today, who owes you money, what stock is low, or what your VAT estimate means. The assistant uses your business data, so the answers become more useful as you record consistently."

## 13. Automations

### Manual Steps

1. Open **More**.
2. Tap **Automations**.
3. Turn on the reminders or summaries you want.
4. Set the daily reminder time.
5. Enable debt reminder suggestions, low stock alerts, weekly summary, or WhatsApp sending where available.
6. Set quiet hours so reminders are not sent at the wrong time.
7. Tap **Save automations**.

### Video Script

"Automations help the app remind you about important actions. You can enable daily reminders, debt reminder suggestions, low stock alerts, weekly summaries, and approved WhatsApp sending. Set quiet hours so messages and reminders stay within a suitable time."

## 14. More: Staff, Security, Billing, Legal, And Sign Out

### Staff Access

Owners can invite staff and accountants. Staff have sales access only. Accountants have reports access only. Owners have full access to money, stock, people, and reports.

### Manual Steps For Staff Invitations

1. Open **More**.
2. Scroll to **Security and operations**.
3. Enter the staff or accountant email address.
4. Choose the role: **Staff** or **Accountant**.
5. Tap **Invite**.
6. Share the invitation link shown in the invitations list if needed.

### Manual Steps For Active Devices

1. Open **More**.
2. Go to **Active devices**.
3. Review signed-in devices.
4. Remove any device that should no longer have access.

### Manual Steps For Backup

1. Open **More**.
2. Go to **Backup and restore**.
3. Tap **Export** if your role allows it.
4. Store the backup securely.

### Manual Steps For Billing And Paid Tools

1. Open **More**.
2. Review **Growth features**.
3. If billing is available, choose a plan or use paid tools such as category assist, receipt extraction, and accountant export.
4. Follow the secure payment screen when upgrading.

### Manual Steps For Legal And Account

1. Open **More**.
2. Open Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Financial Disclaimer, Data Security, Refund Policy, or Delete account request.
3. To leave the app, tap **Sign out**.

### Video Script

"The More screen keeps advanced tools away from daily recording. Here you can view your role, open reports, use the AI Assistant, configure automations, read legal policies, invite staff, manage active devices, export backups, view billing tools, and sign out. Owners have full access, staff have sales access, and accountants focus on reports."

## 15. Offline Use And Sync

### Manual Steps

1. If the app shows that you are offline, you can still save some money and stock changes.
2. Watch for the offline banner near the bottom of the screen.
3. Reconnect to the internet.
4. Tap **Retry** if the app does not sync automatically.
5. Wait for the confirmation message that offline changes have synced.

### Video Script

"If your internet drops, SME MoneyBook can save some changes offline and sync them later. Watch the banner at the bottom of the screen. When your internet returns, the app will try to sync. You can also tap Retry."

## 16. Best Practices For Daily Use

1. Record every sale and expense as soon as it happens.
2. Use Cash, Bank, and POS correctly so available money stays accurate.
3. Use pay-later sales for customers who owe you.
4. Add customer phone numbers when you want WhatsApp reminders or invoices.
5. Add products before selling from stock.
6. Review People daily so debts do not stay unpaid.
7. Review Stock weekly so low stock does not surprise you.
8. Review Reports before business decisions, tax filing, or accountant discussions.
9. Use staff roles instead of sharing the owner password.
10. Sign out on devices you no longer use.
